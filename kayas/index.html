Executive Management Summary

One integrated platform — from physical data center to digital services.

KAYAS is not positioned as a cabinet or hardware purchase. The objective is to combine physical infrastructure, HCI, virtualization, storage, cloud management, data-center networking, security, backup/DR, DCIM and NOC operations into an operable and monetizable service platform. H3C's principal advantage is end-to-end integration and Day-2 operational simplicity rather than an absolute claim of category-by-category superiority.
